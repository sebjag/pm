    Resist Satan’s Tactics​—Trust In Jehovah and In Jesus

    “Oppose the Devil, and he will flee from you.”​—JAS. 4:7.

    [JAS. 4:7]
    7 Therefore, subject yourselves to God;+ but oppose the Devil,+ and he will flee from you.+

    FOCUS

    Don’t be fooled by Satan’s attempts to misrepresent Jehovah God and Jesus Christ.

    1. What tactic does Satan use to try to break the integrity of God’s servants?

    FROM the moment we decide to serve Jehovah, Satan and his demons become our unrelenting enemies. But Satan knows that he cannot overpower us as long as we have Jehovah’s support. So the Devil tries to undermine that support by getting us to turn our back on Jehovah. One way he does this is by trying to make us doubt that Jehovah and his Son are truly on our side.​—2 Cor. 11:14; Jas. 1:6.

    [2 Cor. 11:14;]
    14 And no wonder, for Satan himself keeps disguising himself as an angel of light.+
    [Jas. 1:6]
    6 But let him keep asking in faith,+ not doubting at all,+ for the one who doubts is like a wave of the sea driven by the wind and blown about.+

    2. Why is it important that we have confidence in Jehovah God and in Jesus Christ?

    2 In the midst of a battle, soldiers need to have confidence in their leaders. If they were to lose that confidence, they might act independently. This could cost them their lives. In a similar way, we are soldiers in a spiritual battle against “wicked spirit forces.” (Eph. 6:12) Satan the Devil knows that if we lose confidence in Jehovah God and in Jesus Christ, we will likely reject their lead and support. So he does all he can to weaken our confidence in them. In this article, we will examine how he used this tactic in the past and how he is still using it today. Then we will consider how we can resist his schemes. In the next article, we will examine two other tactics Satan uses against us in our spiritual warfare.a

    [Eph. 6:12]
    12 because we have a struggle,+ not against blood and flesh, but against the governments, against the authorities, against the world rulers of this darkness, against the wicked spirit forces+ in the heavenly places.+
    [Footnote a]

    a Satan does not directly cause all the trials we face. But he is responsible for “the spirit of the world,” which influences opposers to mistreat God’s people. So whether directly or indirectly, Satan is behind many of the challenges we face in our spiritual warfare.​—1 Cor. 2:12; 1 John 5:19.

    SATAN MISREPRESENTED JEHOVAH AND JESUS

    3. How was Eve’s confidence in Jehovah undermined by Satan?

    3 Early in human history, Satan set out to turn Adam and Eve away from Jehovah. Satan deceived Eve into believing that her Creator did not have her best interests at heart. Satan asked her: “Did God really say that you must not eat from every tree of the garden?” (Gen. 3:1-5) Satan convinced Eve that Jehovah was imposing unreasonable limits on both her and her husband. Sadly, Eve and later Adam sided with the enemy Satan. They turned their back on Jehovah​—the One who had given them everything.​—Gen. 3:6.

(Job 22:1-3) The Devil assumed that personal tragedies would cause Job to reject Jehovah. Although Job was under great pressure from Satan’s scheme, that faithful man maintained his integrity, never losing sight of his friendship with God.​—Job 27:1, 5.

    SATAN CONTINUES TO MISREPRESENT JEHOVAH AND JESUS

    8. How does Satan use false religion to spread lies about Jehovah and about Jesus?

    8 Today, one of Satan’s main tactics is to obscure the truth about Jehovah and about Jesus. As “the father of the lie,” the Devil spreads lies about them by means of false religion. (John 8:44) For example, he has blinded people about the true identity of Jehovah and of his Son. (2 Cor. 4:3, 4) Christendom often presents the Father and the Son as being equal. As a result, people tend to ignore the Father, whose name they do not know, and worship the Son. Neither Jehovah nor Jesus approves of such worship. (Matt. 4:10; John 4:23) When people are blinded in such ways, they may be difficult to reason with or may even oppose our ministry.

(Prov. 19:3) Those who begin to feel this way may even wonder: ‘What is the point of continuing to follow Jehovah’s laws and principles? It doesn’t seem to benefit me!’ This type of thinking could lead us to come under Satan’s influence.
